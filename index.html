    How to Play

    Objective

    Guess the mystery cricketer within eight attempts.

    Making a Guess

    1. Search for a cricketer by name.
    2. Select a player from the suggestions.
    3. Compare the revealed attributes against the mystery cricketer.
    4. Use the clues to narrow down your next guess.

    Understanding the Colors

    • Blue: The attribute matches exactly.
    • Amber: The attribute is close or partially overlaps.
    • Gray: The attribute does not match.
    • Neutral: The attribute has missing data.

    Age Arrows

    An upward arrow means the mystery cricketer is older than your guess. A downward arrow means the mystery cricketer is younger.

    Hints

    After five unsuccessful guesses, you may reveal one attribute of the mystery cricketer.

    New Rounds
